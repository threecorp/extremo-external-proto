TenantBookingSetting

TenantBookingSetting is the part of a tenant's settings that shapes its booking

calendar. Hours are wall-clock hours in `time_zone`; a value of 24 or more is

an hour of the next day (26 = 02:00 the next day). Which scheduling strategy

applies is reported by AvailabilityService.ListSlots as `schedule_type`.

FieldTypeLabelDescription
calendar_start int32

First bookable hour of a day (5..28).

calendar_end int32

Hour the shop closes (6..29); a booking may end exactly at this hour.

calendar_interval int32

Slot width in seconds (1800 = 30 minutes).

calendar_slot int32

Bookings a single slot can hold when the strategy counts slots.

auto_accept_booking bool

When true, CreateBooking returns RESERVED; otherwise DRAFT until the shop accepts it.

reservation_note string

Notes the shop asks consumers to read before booking.

time_zone string

IANA time zone the hours are in. Always "Asia/Tokyo" today.

no_show_threshold_minutes int32

Minutes after a booking ends before a booking still RESERVED is marked NO_SHOW automatically (0..1440). 0 turns the automatic marking off.

calendar_sync_attendance bool

When true, booking capacity follows staff shifts (see AttendanceService): a time is bookable only while a staff member is on shift, and calendar_slot, when above 0, caps it further. When false, shifts are ignored and calendar_slot alone sizes each slot. Busy time imported from staff members' connected calendars applies only when true.

AttendanceService

AttendanceService manages a tenant's staff shifts and their breaks, as the

admin UI does: reads (attendance.read), creating (attendance.create), changing

(attendance.update), moving a shift to another staff member

(attendance.reassign) and deleting (attendance.delete).

A shift belongs to a staff member listed by StaffService.ListStaff, may not

overlap another shift of the same staff member, and may span several days. Its

start and its end must each fall within the shop's business hours of their own

day (TenantBookingSetting.calendar_start .. calendar_end, minute precision). A

break must lie within its shift.

Deleting a shift, or moving it to another staff member, removes the previous

staff member as the requested staff of the bookings overlapping the shift's

time before the change, except bookings that are CANCELED or NO_SHOW; the

bookings themselves remain.

An update changes only the fields set in the request, so two updates of

different fields of the same shift both apply. An id of another tenant, or a

break of another shift, returns NOT_FOUND. An invalid value returns

INVALID_ARGUMENT. A write to a shift or its breaks returns ABORTED when another

request moved the shift to another staff member while this one ran, or when it

hits a database deadlock with another edit; read the shift again and retry.

A single service whose total duration (children included) drives slot feasibility. Set this or service_ids, not both.

Start of the date range to compute (inclusive).

closed_at google.protobuf.Timestamp

End of the date range to compute (exclusive).

Optional booking being rescheduled (Booking.id). When set, that booking is not counted, as UpdateBooking does not count it against itself, and the slots are checked for the staff and consumers it keeps, as UpdateBooking checks them.
